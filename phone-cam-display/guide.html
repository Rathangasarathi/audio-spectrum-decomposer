PhoneCam Display

Stream your phone's camera to your laptop screen wirelessly — in HD quality, with near-zero latency.

⚡ Requires: Both phone and laptop must be on the same WiFi network. Uses WebRTC for peer-to-peer streaming — no server needed, no data leaves your network.

🚀 Open Laptop Display →

📋 How to Use

1

Open Laptop Display

Open index.html in your laptop browser (Chrome recommended). You'll see a 4-digit session code.

2

Send phone.html to Your Phone

Send the phone.html file to your phone via WhatsApp, email, AirDrop, Bluetooth file transfer, or USB. Open it in Chrome mobile.

3

Enter the Session Code

On your phone, type the 4-digit code shown on the laptop screen and tap "Connect to Laptop".

4

Allow Camera Permission

When your phone browser asks, allow camera access. Your camera stream will instantly appear on the laptop screen!

5

Enjoy your stream 🎉

Switch between front/rear cameras, take snapshots, mirror the video, or go fullscreen on the laptop.
